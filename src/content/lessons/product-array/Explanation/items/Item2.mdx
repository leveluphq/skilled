To improve on the brute force solution, what if we captured these patterns and repeated operations.

We know that for any item `results[i]`, we can calculate the answer from the product of all the items to the left and right.
Arrays have constant time <BigO.constant /> lookup for indexes, so what if we created additional lookup arrays that would give us the left and right products at that index.

We could accomplish this by adding two new arrays - one that stores the left products and another that stores the right products.
Then the answer at any index would just be the running product from the right and left multiplied together.

Think how you would construct these lookup arrays.
