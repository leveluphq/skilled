What does it mean for an element to be the product of every item in the array except for itself?

If we have an array of 6 numbers, the product at any given index will be the product of 5 items, or all the items other than the one at the given index.

<ExampleProducts />

This is really just our brute force solution written out explicitly.
For each item in the results, we find the product of every other item other than the one at that index.

Written out like this, we can see there is a lot of repeated work.
We do the same multiplication over and over again.

It also looks like we may have a pattern here.

Not only are we doing the same multiplication repeatedly, we are doing it in the same order.

So when we say something is the product of all the other items in the array,
what this actually means is that it is the product of all the items to the left of it and the right of it.
