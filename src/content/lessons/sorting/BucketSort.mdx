When the data take a fixed range of values, it can be divided into pre-defined buckets, and in this case you can actually sort in <BigO.n letter="n" /> time.
You perform one pass over the data to fill your buckets, and then one additional pass over the buckets to form your output.

> This is similar to radix sort, but bucket sort is a little more simple to conceptualize and implement. Creating simple buckets is likely all you will need in an interview.
