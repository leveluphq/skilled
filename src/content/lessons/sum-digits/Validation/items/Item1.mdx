The input is a single integer, so think about what actually grows as it gets larger: its number of digits.
For example, the largest unsigned 64-bit integer is `2^64 - 1 = 18,446,744,073,709,551,615`, which has 20 total digits.
Your Big O will be in terms of the number of digits <BigO.n letter="d" />,
not the value of the integer.
