Imagine you are given a list of 1000 items on a piece of paper, and you want to find all the matching target pairs by hand.
You would probably start at the first item,
determine the number that would match it to sum to the target,
and then walk through the list to look for this matching value.
Then you would repeat this process for each item in the list until you reached the end.

Seeing something like this should trigger you to recognize that it's a brute force solution.
That is, `for` each item in the input array, we loop through the array to compare it to every other item to find the answer.
Or in code, it would require nested `for` loops that lead to a horrible quadratic time complexity <BigO.n2 />.
