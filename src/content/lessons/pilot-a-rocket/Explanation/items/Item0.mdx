Are there any other assumptions we can make about our graph?
Although it doesn't state it, we can likely assume that since edges represent a channel of communication, that it will work in both directions.
This would mean our graph is <ConceptModal content={<DirectedVsUndirected />}>undirected</ConceptModal>.
There isn't a reference to paths taking any more or less effort to communicate, so we can also assume our graph is <ConceptModal content={<WeightedVsUnweighted />}>unweighted</ConceptModal>.
