What makes strings unique is that there is a limited set of characters (eg. 26 in the English alphabet),
and their order and number of times a character appears conveys meaning.
For string interview questions, it's very common to test on this through palindromes, permutations, and anagrams.
Having an understanding of what these terms mean before going in can help you feel comfortable diving into a problem.
