Your initial plan may be to find the difference between the smallest and largest value in the input array.
Since the input prices are sequential days, it will fail anytime the largest price comes before the smallest price
because it would indicate that we should buy in the future and sell in the past, which unfortunately we can't do until time travel is invented.
