We saw that we can solve this question by using a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greedy_algorithm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">greedy algorithm</a>.

We'll track two variables, the <MaxProfit /> and the <MinPrice />.

The steps we follow are: prices

1. Initialize <MaxProfit /> to 0
2. Initialize <MinPrice /> to the price of the first item in the array
3. Iterate through each price in the input
4. Set the max profit to either the previous max profit or the profit of selling the current <MinPrice /> at this value
5. Update the <MinPrice /> to either be the current <MinPrice /> or the price at the day we're on

Following these steps, we ensure that we're buying at the lowest price and selling at the highest price based on the available information at the current day.
