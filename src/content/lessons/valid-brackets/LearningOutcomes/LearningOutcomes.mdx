We gained intuition on when to use a stack by recognizing that we only needed to compare a closing bracket with the most recently seen opening bracket.

An understanding of when to use a stack may be tricky at first, but it comes quickly with practice.
If you make the realization that you need the "most recent", "top item", "current minimum/maximum value",
that's an indication that using a stack could be the best choice.
